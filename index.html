Cultural Soul of Columbio

Honoring Blaan Indigenous Heritage & Living Traditions

Long before visitors marveled at Columbio's rolling emerald ridges, the indigenous Blaan people nurtured these highlands with deep reverence for nature. Today, Columbio's community-based eco-tourism empowers Blaan elders, artisans, forest guards, and local youth as primary stewards of their ancestral landscape.

Tabih Weaving & Beadwork

Intricate abaca ikat textiles and traditional brass-and-bead accessories crafted by local Blaan weavers.

Kasfala & Cultural Festivals

Celebrations of peace, thanksgiving harvests, agung gong ensembles, and traditional Blaan dances.

Always ask permission before photographing elders & sacred sites.

Responsible Cultural Tourism Pledge

100% of environmental and guide fees go directly to Barangay eco-tourism associations and indigenous community development funds.

Official Municipal Tourism Advisory

Sustainable Tourism & Safety Protocols

All Major Eco-Sites Open (Pre-Registration Advised)

300-Visitor Daily Carrying Cap

To protect the fragile cogonal grass ecosystem of La Palmera Mountain Ridge, LGU Columbio enforces a strict 300 visitors/day limit. Register at the Poblacion Tourism Desk or online before ascending.

Accredited Guides & Skylab Riders

For your safety on steep mountain ridges, caves, and river trails, only hire LGU-accredited tour guides and registered Skylab/4x4 operators wearing official municipal ID badges.

Leave No Trace & Fire Safety

Carry out all single-use plastics and trash. Open bonfires and cigarette smoking are strictly prohibited on dry grassland ridges to prevent grassfires.
